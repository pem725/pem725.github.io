Impact across the whole department

Every full-time faculty member in the George Mason University Department of Psychology — professors, instructors, everyone — measured the same way from two independent bibliometric databases. Two things this shows that a hand-picked list never could: how concentrated the department's research impact really is, and how much any one person's "number" depends on which database you cite.
